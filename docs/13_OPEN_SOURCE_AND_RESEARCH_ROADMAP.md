## Research paper opportunities

Potential paper directions:

1. Multimodal extraction of borehole and geological-section information from unstructured geotechnical reports.
2. Provenance-aware geological knowledge representation from engineering reports.
3. Uncertainty-aware spatial reconstruction from sparse borehole evidence.
4. Constraint-aware multimodal geological digital twins.

The strongest combined paper/product story is:

> A traceable multimodal pipeline that reconstructs a subsurface model while explicitly separating observations from inferred geometry and uncertainty.

## Dataset strategy

Prefer public reports and project-independent examples. Store annotations in a schema that can be released separately from source documents if copyright restrictions prevent redistribution.
